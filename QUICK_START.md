# AcerbE™ — Quick Start Guide
### Military-Grade Digital Fingerprinter

---

## What AcerbE™ Does

AcerbE™ invisibly brands every digital product you sell with a unique fingerprint
tied to the buyer. If they share or resell your product, you can scan any copy
and instantly identify exactly who bought it and when.

---

## Installation — 2 Steps Only

### Step 1 — Install Python (free, one time)
- Go to **https://python.org**
- Click the yellow "Download Python" button
- Run the installer
- **Windows users:** check the box that says "Add Python to PATH"

### Step 2 — Launch AcerbE™
- **Mac:** Double-click **Launch_AcerbE.command**
  *(Right-click → Open the first time if Mac asks for permission)*
- **Windows:** Double-click **Launch_AcerbE.bat**

Your browser opens automatically. AcerbE™ is ready to use.

---

## How to Stamp a Product

1. Click **Stamp Product** in the left menu
2. Fill in the buyer's name, email, and your transaction ID
3. Enter your product name and ID
4. Drop your product file into the upload area
5. Click **Stamp Fingerprint**
6. Download the stamped `.rpx` file — **deliver THIS to the buyer, not the original**

---

## How to Catch a Pirate

1. Click **Forensic Scan** in the left menu
2. Drop the suspected pirated file into the upload area
3. Click **Run Forensic Scan**
4. AcerbE™ will show you exactly who bought that copy and when

## Important — Keep These Safe

- The **`acerbe_registry.db`** file inside the AcerbE™ folder is your evidence database.
  Back it up regularly. This is your proof of every sale.
- The **buyer key** shown after each stamp — copy it somewhere safe.
  You need it to decrypt files if a buyer ever needs support.

---

## Stopping AcerbE™

Close the terminal/command window that opened when you launched.
Your browser tab will stop working — that is normal.
