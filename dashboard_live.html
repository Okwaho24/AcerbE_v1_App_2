Protection Layers
LAYER 1 — STEGANOGRAPHY
Format-specific invisible embedding. LSB pixel injection (images), XMP metadata (PDF), trailing bytes (audio), manifest + inner marking (ZIP). Undetectable to the naked eye or ear.
LAYER 2 — CRYPTO BINDING
AES-256-CBC encryption with a PBKDF2-derived key unique to every buyer + transaction. Mathematically impossible for any two buyers to share a key.
LAYER 3 — TAMPER GUARD
HMAC-SHA256 wrapper over the full encrypted file. Any modification — even a single byte — invalidates the HMAC. Extraction produces a zero-byte unusable blob.
LAYER 4 — FORENSIC REGISTRY
Every issued fingerprint logged to an encrypted local SQLite database with HMAC-sealed records. Buyer name, email, transaction ID, product, and timestamp — all tamper-evident.
