────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. SCOPE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This policy governs data handling within AcerbE™.

AcerbE™ is a local-only software application.
This policy applies to all versions and tiers of AcerbE™.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. NO DATA COLLECTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Archer Chain Analytics™ does not collect any data through
AcerbE™.

AcerbE™ does not:

- Collect user data, usage data, or behavioral data.
- Transmit any data to Archer Chain Analytics™ or any
  third party.
- Maintain any external database, server, or cloud service.
- Record installation events, session data, or error logs
  externally.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. NO TELEMETRY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
AcerbE™ contains no telemetry.

No analytics, crash reporting, feature tracking, or usage
monitoring is implemented.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. NO EXTERNAL TRANSMISSION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
AcerbE™ does not initiate any outbound network connection.

All operations are executed locally on your system.
All data remains on your system.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. NO CLOUD DEPENDENCY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
AcerbE™ requires no cloud account, cloud storage, or
external API.

AcerbE™ operates fully offline.
Internet access is not required for any core function.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. LOCAL DATA — YOUR RESPONSIBILITY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
AcerbE™ stores the following locally on your system:

- The Registry database (acerbe_registry.db) containing
  fingerprint records, buyer identifiers, and transaction
  references that you enter.
- Stamped output files produced during your operations.
- Temporary upload files cleared after processing.

You are solely responsible for:

- The security of all locally stored data.
- Compliance with PIPEDA and applicable provincial
  privacy legislation regarding any personal data you
  enter into AcerbE™.
- Lawful handling of buyer information stored in the
  Registry.
- Backup and recovery of Registry data.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. BUYER DATA — OPERATOR OBLIGATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
If you use AcerbE™ to process buyer information (name,
email, transaction ID), you are the data controller.

Archer Chain Analytics™ is not the data controller for
any buyer information you process using AcerbE™.

You must:

- Maintain a compliant privacy policy for your own buyers.
- Obtain lawful basis for processing buyer personal data.
- Respond to any data subject requests from your buyers.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. THIRD-PARTY DEPENDENCIES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
AcerbE™ uses the following open-source Python libraries,
which operate locally and do not transmit data:

- Pillow (image processing)
- cryptography (AES-256, PBKDF2, HMAC)
- pikepdf (PDF metadata)

No third-party service accounts are required.
No third-party APIs are called.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Archer Chain Analytics™ may update this policy with new
versions of AcerbE™.

Updates will be distributed with the updated software
package.
